03

The practical stuff

Transcripts, degree certificate, CV and proof of funding — straightforward, but start early.

Academic transcripts

An official record of your bachelor's grades, ideally with an explanation of your university's grading scale for international readers.

Degree certificate

Or proof of expected graduation — most schools accept a letter from your university confirming your expected graduation date.

CV / résumé

One to two pages. Even "0 years experience" programmes want to see internships, projects, and leadership activities.

Proof of funding

Bank statements, a scholarship award letter, or a loan approval — most visa applications require evidence you can cover tuition and living costs.

04

Statement of purpose / essays

How to write yours, with tips, examples and further reading.

Quick tips

  • Start 2–3 months out — the best essays go through 4–5 drafts, not one.
  • Answer the actual question asked, not a generic "why an MBA" essay recycled for every school.
  • Show, don't tell: one concrete story beats three abstract claims about your "leadership."
  • Tailor each essay to that specific programme — name real courses, clubs, or professors where relevant.
  • Get at least one outside read from someone who'll be honest, not just encouraging.

Resources & examples

  • Poets&Quants — essay tips, admitted-student essay examples, and school-specific guidance.
  • Clear Admit — essay analysis and admissions consultant directory.
  • mbaMission Insider's Guides — free school-by-school application guides.
  • Search YouTube for "[school name] admissions webinar" — most schools post their own official essay-writing sessions.
05

Letters of recommendation

How to choose recommenders and set them up to write a strong letter.

Quick tips

  • Pick recommenders who know your work closely — a direct manager beats a more senior name who barely knows you.
  • Ask at least 4–6 weeks before the deadline, never less than two.
  • Send them a "brag sheet": your CV, your target programmes, and 2–3 specific examples they could reference.
  • At least one academic reference where possible, especially for fresh-graduate (MiM-style) programmes.
  • Follow up a week before the deadline — a friendly reminder, not a nag.

Resources & examples

  • mba.com Recommender Guidelines — what most schools' recommendation forms actually ask.
  • Poets&Quants — school-specific recommendation question examples.
  • Give your recommender a draft "outline" of themes to cover — it saves them time and improves the letter.

Our mission

Why we built Campus Atlas.

Choosing a master's abroad usually means fifteen browser tabs, a dozen PDFs, and no easy way to compare tuition, deadlines, or eligibility side by side. Campus Atlas puts all of it in one place.

What we do

We map pre-experience and early-career master's programmes at leading universities worldwide — across business, engineering, science, humanities and more — pulling together tuition, duration, entry requirements, deadlines, and — increasingly rare to find in one place — realistic post-study work rights.

What we don't do

We're not an agency and we don't take commission from schools. Figures shown are indicative starting points, not official numbers — always confirm details on a school's own admissions page before applying.

Where this is headed

More programmes, more countries, and sharper matching against your own academic background, so the list you see reflects where you actually stand a chance — not just where you'd like to go.
